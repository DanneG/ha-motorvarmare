## Optional evening cable check

- Enable it per car with a W or kW power sensor. Choose the evening time (default 21:00), or use the **Test heater cable** button in an automation.
- Before a departure tomorrow that needs heating, the plug briefly switches on. A fresh low-power reading can trigger a cable reminder. Missing readings give an inconclusive result.
- The card’s **Settings** tab shows the last result. After connecting the cable, you can repeat the check after one minute. A successful check clears the Home Assistant reminder.

Update in Marketplace or HACS, restart Home Assistant between heating sessions and reload the dashboard. For YAML dashboard resources, change the card URL version to `0.14.0`. The check is off until you enable it.
